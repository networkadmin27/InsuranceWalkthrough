Instructions

  1. Log into your Google Workspace Admin console
  2. Expand the Apps drop-down
  3. Expand the Google Workspace drop-down and click Gmail
  4. Click Compliance
  5. In the Organizational Units section toward the middle-left of the page, ensure your domain is selected
  6. Scroll to the Content Compliance section
  7. If no rule has been set up before, click Configure. Otherwise, click Add Another Rule
  8. Give the Content Compliance rule a name
  9. Under Email messages to affect, check the Inbound box
  10. Under Add expressions that describe the content you want to search for in each message, click ADD:
    • Select Advanced content match from the drop-down list
    • Change Location to Envelope sender. You may need to scroll down to see the option
    • Change Match type to Matches regex
    • In the Regexp text box, enter the expression
    • Click SAVE
  11. Under the 3. If the above expressions match, do the following section, scroll to Spam
  12. Check the Bypass spam filter for this message box
  13. Click SAVE
