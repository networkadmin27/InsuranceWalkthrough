Instructions

  1. Please note that this requires you to have the Security Administrator role group
  2. Log into your Microsoft 365 admin center
  3. Expand the Admin centers drop-down
  4. Click Security to open the Microsoft Defender admin center
  5. Expand the Email & collaboration drop-down
  6. Click Policies & rules and select Threat policies
  7. Select the Advanced Delivery option under the Rules section
  8. Click the Phishing simulation tab near the top
  9. Then click Edit directly underneath where the tab was on the page
  10. In the popup, expand the Domain section
  11. Click in the box and type out our domains to add them to the list
  12. Next, expand the Sending IP section
  13. Click in the box and type out our IPs to add them to the list
  14. Finally, expand the Simulation URLs to allow section
  15. Click in the box and add
  16. Click Save
